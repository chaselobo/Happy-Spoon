# Happy Spoon

Protein yogurt in dessert flavors with good macros. Working brand name: Happy Spoon.

## Chosen branding: Concept 3 "Loud Mouth"
Read `branding/concept-3-loud-mouth/brand-spec.md` before any branding, packaging, naming or copy work. It has the flavor names, exact colors, fonts, tub layout and voice.

- Visual reference: `branding/concept-3-loud-mouth/tubs.png`
- Editable source: `branding/concept-3-loud-mouth/tubs.html`
- Photoreal tub mockups: `branding/concept-3-loud-mouth/realistic-mockups/` (prompts in `generation-prompts.json`; copies in `assets/`)
- Flavor lineup: Cocoa Loco (chocolate), Mint Condition (mint chip), Cara Meltdown (salted caramel), Tough Cookie (cookies & cream)
