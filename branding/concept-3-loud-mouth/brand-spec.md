# Happy Spoon — Concept 3: Loud Mouth (chosen direction)

Bold, cheeky, Goodles-style packaging for a dessert-flavored protein yogurt. Positioned for **dessert lovers who want less guilt**, not the gym crowd.

- Visual: [tubs.png](tubs.png) (static image) · [tubs.html](tubs.html) (open in a browser)
- Photoreal mockups (v1, AI-generated from tubs.png): [realistic-mockups/](realistic-mockups/) — one PNG per flavor, prompts in `generation-prompts.json`. Duplicate copies in `/assets/`. Notes: text renders correctly; mockups use a heavier/wider bold type than Anton; tub shape reads as a quart ice cream tub (real 32 oz yogurt tubs are squatter); starburst is low-contrast on the caramel tub.
- Live design canvas (private, all 4 original concepts): https://claude.ai/artifact/7sV5WKGH88QGByiEZzYjti

**Tastes like cheating. Isn't.**

"HAPPY SPOON" wordmark sits inside the drip band, in a color that contrasts with the drip (brown on cream, mint-white on chocolate, toffee on pink, cream on black).

## Tub layout (top to bottom)
1. Lid in the drip color; the sauce drips down from the lid over the top of the tub
2. **HAPPY SPOON** wordmark in the drip band (Anton, spaced caps)
3. Pun name, huge, two lines, all caps (Anton)
4. Real flavor name, small caps (Archivo ExtraBold) — always shown so shoppers know what it is
5. Bottom row: red-orange starburst sticker **20g PROTEIN** + black tape label **0g ADDED SUGAR** + "PROTEIN YOGURT" + net weight

## Shared elements
- Starburst sticker: `#FF4F2E` with dark `#1B1512` text (open question: may blend on the caramel tub)
- Tape label: `#1B1512` background, white text, slightly rotated
- Board / brand background: sunshine yellow `#FFE45C`, ink `#1B1512`
- Net weight line: `NET WT 32 OZ (2 LB) 907g`

## Type
- **Anton** (Google Fonts) — pun names, wordmark, headlines. Always caps.
- **Archivo ExtraBold** (Google Fonts) — stickers, labels, small copy.

## Voice
Cheeky, confident, a little rebellious. Short lines.
- "WARNING: may cause aggressive spoon-licking."
- "Dessert called. We picked up with 20g of protein."
- "Zero added sugar. Zero chill."

## Naming rules
- Everyday phrase with the flavor hidden inside (Mint Condition, Tough Cookie, Cocoa Loco, Cara Meltdown).
- Two lines, ~10 letters per line max.
- Salted caramel name should lean on *caramel*, not *salt* (user rejected salt-based names).
- Avoid anything echoing other brands' trademarks (Oreo, Cookie Monster, Salt Life, Coca-Cola — hence "Cocoa" not "Coca").
- Still to do: USPTO trademark search on all four names.
